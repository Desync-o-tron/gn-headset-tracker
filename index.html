Times and weights come from the Aug 2026 build plan: frame weights include ~50% support/raft overhead, and every “to go” figure carries the 10% failure buffer. A headset counts as complete when every part for it is printed — 4 siliconces, outer + inner frame, 2 sponges, 4 snaps, 2 conductive + 2 PLA sliders, 1 stopper. Log failed prints with the fail button so scrap shows up in the totals — fail also uses the typed amount (empty = 1). Type any amount in the box and hit add (or Enter) to log a batch; an empty box adds one typical plate — that's the greyed number. Counts are shared: everyone with the link sees and updates the same numbers, refreshed about every 15 seconds.
